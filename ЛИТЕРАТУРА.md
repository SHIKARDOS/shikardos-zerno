## Экономическая теория инноваций

1. Schumpeter, J. A. *The Theory of Economic Development: An Inquiry into Profits, Capital, Credit, Interest, and the Business Cycle.* Harvard University Press, 1934. (Немецкий оригинал — 1912.)
