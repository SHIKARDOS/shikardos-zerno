# BIBLIOGRAPHY (preliminary list)

*The list accumulates as the chapters are written. First edition; it will be expanded and verified in the preparation of the final version.*

## Economic theory of innovation

1. Schumpeter, J. A. *The Theory of Economic Development: An Inquiry into Profits, Capital, Credit, Interest, and the Business Cycle.* Harvard University Press, 1934. (German original — 1912.)

## Knowledge recombination and innovation

2. Xiao, T., Makhija, M., Karim, S. *A Knowledge Recombination Perspective of Innovation: Review and New Research Directions.* Journal of Management, 2022, 48(6). DOI: 10.1177/01492063211055982.
3. Wang, F. *Does the recombination of distant scientific knowledge generate valuable inventions? An analysis of pharmaceutical patents.* Technovation, 2024, 130, 102947. DOI: 10.1016/j.technovation.2023.102947.

## Engineering methodologies of invention

4. Petrov, V. *TRIZ. Theory of Inventive Problem Solving.* Springer, 2019. DOI: 10.1007/978-3-030-04254-7.
5. Altshuller, G. S. *Find an Idea: Introduction to TRIZ — the Theory of Inventive Problem Solving.* (mentioned in Chapters 2, 5, 6 as the source of the systematization of invention).

## Cognitive psychology of creativity

6. Finke, R. A., Ward, T. B., Smith, S. M. *Creative Cognition: Theory, Research, and Applications.* MIT Press, 1992.

## Philosophical foundations

7. Aristotle. *Metaphysics* (the notions of potency and act, entelechy — Chapter 3).
8. Hegel, G. W. F. *The Science of Logic* (the notion of sublation/Aufhebung — Chapter 3).
9. Bacon, F. *The New Organon* (the method of the systematic collection of facts and induction — Chapter 2).

---

*Note: the list is preliminary. Exact bibliographic data and completeness will be verified at the stage of editing; individual philosophical sources are given without publishing details and require clarification.*
